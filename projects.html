Wearable IMU for Movement Assessment

Upper-Limb Movement Detection and Gait Analysis in Neuromuscular Disease

Deep learning methods for point-to-point (P2P) upper-limb movement detection and impairment assessment from continuous IMU streams, plus wearable accelerometry to quantify gait stability, postural sway, and six-minute-walk performance in neuromuscular disorders and pediatric-onset multiple sclerosis.

Research Contributions

  • ▸P2P upper-limb movement detection from continuous IMU streams (IEEE ICHI 2026)
  • ▸Gait stability and postural-sway analysis from 6-Minute Walk Test accelerometry
  • ▸Longitudinal training and evaluation workflow for neuromuscular datasets

Multitask Learning for Intervention Readiness

Joint Modeling of Availability and Emotion-Regulation Desire in Cancer Survivors

A multitask deep learning framework that jointly models intervention availability and emotion-regulation desire in cancer survivors from smartphone passive sensing and EMA — helping mHealth systems deliver support when a person is both available and receptive.

Research Contributions

  • ▸Multitask deep learning over smartphone sensing (accelerometer, GPS, motion) and EMA
  • ▸Joint prediction of intervention availability and emotion-regulation desire
  • ▸Participant-level cross-validation; presented as a poster at UVA OVPR (2025)

PULSE

Agentic Investigation with Passive Sensing for Proactive Intervention in Cancer Survivorship

PULSE is a single-agent LLM framework that performs agentic investigation over longitudinal passive-sensing streams via structured tool calls, supporting proactive intervention in cancer survivorship. I contributed to schema design over multimodal sensor data and to evaluation against monolithic and multi-task baselines.

Research Contributions

  • ▸Single-agent LLM that investigates multimodal sensor streams through structured tool calls
  • ▸Schema design over longitudinal passive-sensing data for agentic reasoning
  • ▸Evaluation against monolithic and multi-task baselines

HealthGraph

Grounded In-Context Reasoning for Endocrine Therapy Adherence

A grounded in-context reasoning approach that replaces retrieval-augmented generation with subject-specific structured evidence packets, using in-context learning and chain-of-thought to generate personalized weekly adherence-intervention messages for breast cancer survivors on oral endocrine therapy (multimodal Fitbit, MEMS, and EMA data).

Research Contributions

  • ▸Grounded in-context reasoning with subject-specific structured evidence packets, in place of RAG
  • ▸Personalized weekly adherence messages from multimodal Fitbit, MEMS, and EMA data
  • ▸FActScore-style LLM-as-judge faithfulness evaluation (claude-haiku-4-5, gpt-4o-mini)

Tomoni

On-Device Agentic AI for Postpartum Mental Health Support

A tool-using LLM agent on Android (Kotlin / Jetpack Compose, Claude Sonnet) that lets a postpartum user query her own EMA, journal, and symptom history in natural language through an 8-tool surface and a ReAct-style loop — backed by an on-device RAG pipeline and a strict privacy boundary.

Research Contributions

  • ▸On-device RAG (all-MiniLM-L6-v2 via ONNX Runtime) over SQLCipher-encrypted Room storage
  • ▸8-tool agent surface with a ReAct-style reasoning loop (Claude Sonnet)
  • ▸“Retrieve, do not interpret” safety constraint; N=1 in-situ two-week deployment
